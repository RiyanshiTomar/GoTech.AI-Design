# Natural lighting

HEURISTICS
* Window area around 10-15 % of room floor area for habitable rooms.
* Orientation only when the user gives it: north is +y (top of the plan). Without a stated orientation, do not assume sun direction.
* Living and bedrooms favour the longer exterior wall for windows.
* Deep rooms (aspect > 2.2:1) are poorly lit: ROOM_PROPORTION warning.
